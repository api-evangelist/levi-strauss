Today, Albertsons® Companies, one of the largest food and drug retailers in the U.S., announced that it has named Levi Strauss & Co. Chief Financial and Growth Officer Harmit Singh to its board of directors. “I am honored to join Albertsons’ Board of Directors,” said Harmit.
